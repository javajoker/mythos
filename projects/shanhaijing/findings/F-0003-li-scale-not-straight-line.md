# F-0003：山經里數不是直線距離；比例在篇內一致、篇間不同 / Mountain-Classic 里 are not straight-line distances; the ratio is stable within a section, different between sections

| 撤回條件 / Retraction | H-0002 被否定，或者 T-105 以見證本讀法重抽後比例不再一致 / H-0002 refuted, or the ratio stops being consistent after T-105 |

**繁中**：在 H-0002 的實際錨點下，一個「里」在北次三經對應約 93–136 m 的直線位移，在西山經之首、西次四經對應約 243–254 m。若當時一里約 400 m（`needs-citation`），則文本里數約為直線距離的 1.6–4 倍。同一篇之內，比例相當一致，說明里數是系統性的記錄（可能是實際行走路程），而不是隨意的數字。

**EN**: Under the H-0002 anchors, one stated 里 equals about 93–136 m of straight-line displacement in 北次三經 and about 243–254 m in 西山經之首/西次四經. If the period's 里 was about 400 m (`needs-citation`), stated distances are 1.6–4 times the straight line. The ratio is consistent within a section, so the distances are systematic records (plausibly walking distance), not arbitrary.

**方法影響 / Method impact**：規格 §6.4 預設的 250–600 m 合理範圍，是以「里數 = 直線距離」為前提。未來的卷宗應改用**各篇自行校準的比例**，並在鏈條擬合中把里長下限放寬到約 90 m。這項修改只適用於之後新登記的卷宗，不追溯改動 H-0002 的計分。/ The 250–600 m band in spec §6.4 assumes stated distance = straight line. Future dossiers should use **per-section calibrated ratios** and widen the lower 里 bound to about 90 m in chain fits. This applies only to dossiers registered from now on; it does not retroactively change H-0002's score.
