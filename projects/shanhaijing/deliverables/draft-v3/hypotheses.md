# 篇段層級的地區檢驗 / Section-level region tests

每一篇的山全部放在某一地區，其餘全書重新擬合（ICM 與區塊調整，從基準對應出發），記錄總分。ΔE = 與該篇最佳地區的差。這是局部擬合，不是完整重解：用來比較，不是絕對值。
Each section's mountains are held in one region and the rest of the book is re-fitted (ICM and block moves from the baseline). ΔE is the loss against the section's best region. A local re-fit, for comparison.
