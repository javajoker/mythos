## 中文

### 這是什麼

全書 18 卷、**869 段**，每一段裡的每一個實體（**4,004 次提及**、**1,941 個實體**）都逐條列出：真實世界的對應、可信度、依據；沒有對應的，寫明原因。沒有遺漏，完整性檢查見 [coverage.md](coverage.md)（PASS）。

方法五步（名稱只是符號，不以今名比對）：

1. 找出全書所有實體並合併（山、水、地、國族、生物、植物、礦物、神人）；
2. 取每個實體的描述、屬性與實體之間的關係（相鄰、發源、注入、潛入、產、位於、望見…），成為知識圖譜（2,750 條關係）；
3. 為每個實體在全球找真實候選（31 個地區、135 個水體、116 種動物、47 個古文化），可多個；
4. 用關係交叉驗證：同時為所有實體選候選，使「描述相符 + 關係相符」的總分最高（模擬退火 + 區塊調整），再以丟棄 20% 關係的重解測試穩定性；可信度 = 穩定度 × (1 − e^(−領先幅度))；
5. 產出本初稿。

### 結論與限制

- **山經的骨架可定位**：山與水的相鄰、發源、注入關係把五藏山經主體放在黃河、渭河流域及其周邊，這是全書最強的訊號。
- **全球對應證據不足**：海經、大荒經的條目多只有「在某某東」之類的相對方位，候選地多得無法區分；全球範圍的對應在這一層大多停在「暫定」。這是證據的實際狀態，不是預設。
- 限制：真實世界資料庫的粒度（支流列得多的河流會吸引更多對應，已用唯一性懲罰抵消一部分）；抽取錯誤（自動斷詞，見覆蓋檢查）；多峰（不同起點的解能量相差約 1%）；距離只作小權重參考；「玉」等礦物詞義有詮釋成分。

## English

### What this is

Every one of the **869 paragraphs** of the 18 books, and every entity in each (**4,004 mentions**, **1,941 entities**), with its real-world mapping, confidence and basis, or the reason it is not mapped. Nothing is skipped; see [coverage.md](coverage.md) (PASS).

Five steps (names are symbols, never matched to modern names): (1) extract and merge all entities; (2) descriptions, properties and relations into a knowledge graph (2,750 relations); (3) worldwide candidates per entity (31 regions, 135 waters, 116 animals, 47 ancient cultures); (4) cross-validate: choose all mappings jointly to maximise fit of descriptions and relations (simulated annealing plus block moves), and measure stability by re-solving with 20% of relations dropped; confidence = stability × (1 − e^(−margin)); (5) this draft.

### How to read

`book/` per book and paragraph; `entities.md`; per-type tables; `world-map.svg`. Tiers: high ≥ 0.6, medium ≥ 0.3, low ≥ 0.1, **tentative < 0.1** (best-scoring pick, but re-solves disagree: do not cite).

### Main results

- **Mountain Classics:** of the 172 mountains mapped at ≥ 0.1, 126 (73%) fall in northern and central China, then Tarim 19 and Tibet 13.
- **河** → Yellow River (0.27; alternatives Yangtze 30%, Nile 20%). 渭, 涇, 汾 → Wei, Beiluo, Luo (all in the Yellow River system, consistent with 「注于河」).
- **昆侖之丘** → Tibetan Plateau (0.25; northern/central China 23%). Egypt is not supported.
- **羬羊** → takin (0.67). **毛民** → Ainu (0.92). **玄股** → Arctic peoples (0.31).
- **Sea and Great Wilds books:** peoples and the four seas are mostly tentative; the text gives too few relations to place them.

Tiers of the 879 mapped entities: 5 high, 51 medium, 203 low, 620 tentative. The rest: insufficient evidence 306, no fitting counterpart 256 (mostly composite beings), non-geographic 180, lexical (plants, minerals) 78, minor streams via relations 72, and others.

### Conclusions and limits

The skeleton of the Mountain Classics is locatable: its adjacency, source and mouth relations place it in and around the Yellow and Wei basins. That is the strongest signal in the book. A global mapping of the Sea and Great Wilds books is not supported by the evidence at this level: their entries give mostly relative bearings, which many places satisfy equally well. Limits: the granularity of the world base (rivers with many listed tributaries attract mappings, partly offset by a uniqueness penalty), extraction errors, multimodality (solutions from different starts differ by about 1% in energy), distances used only as a small-weight reference, and interpretive glosses such as 玉 = jade.
