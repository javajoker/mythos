## 6. 紅隊 / Red team
1. **反例**：桂林／番禺、鴈門山兩處方位錯誤；鬱水流向錯誤，都已計入。/ Counter-examples: three errors, all counted.
2. **替身**：如果把這些句子放到任何其他地區（例如地中海世界），地名本身就不存在，無法比對；虛無假說（純想像）無法解釋 11/12 的匯流點正確率。/ Decoy: elsewhere the names do not exist; pure invention cannot explain 11/12 correct confluences.
3. **非獨立性**：這些地名的現代位置，部分是依據同一批漢代文獻確定的（例如《漢書・地理志》）。但匯流點是可以獨立觀察的水文事實，不受這一點影響。/ Non-independence: some modern locations derive from the same Han sources, but confluences are independently observable hydrology.
4. **校改**：無。/ None.
5. **敏感度**：移除 HYD → +0.7，L1；把 GEO 降為 E1 → +1.4，L2。/ Sensitivity: drop HYD → +0.7, L1; GEO to E1 → +1.4, L2.

## 7. 裁決 / Verdict

**L2（有據）。** 海內四經及海內經，特別是海內東經末的水道表，是一份**秦至西漢的地理知識記錄**。它的範圍西至大夏、月氏（今阿富汗北部、烏茲別克南部），北至匈奴，東北至朝鮮與倭（日本），南至海南與南海沿岸，西南至巴蜀。大多數相對方位與匯流點是正確的。
**L2 (supported).** The four 海內 books and the 海內經, above all the waterway list ending 海內東經, are a **Qin–Western Han geographic record**. Its horizon reaches Bactria and the Yuezhi in the west (northern Afghanistan / southern Uzbekistan), the Xiongnu in the north, Korea and Wa (Japan) in the north-east, Hainan and the South China Sea coast in the south, and Ba–Shu in the south-west. Most relational statements and confluences are correct.

**對「全球說」的含意 / Implication for "global" readings**：這一層中最遠的可核實地名是大夏與倭；沒有任何一條可以核實到美洲、歐洲或非洲。/ The most distant checkable names in this layer are Bactria and Wa; none can be verified as reaching the Americas, Europe or Africa.
